Chenxin Li | 李宸鑫

I am a PhD from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK).

I build agentic systems for visual coding, computer use, phone use, robot use, etc., working toward general-purpose multimodal agency.

Previously, I worked as a research intern at ByteDance Seed, Meta MSL, Tencent Hunyuan, etc. Beyond research, I share perspectives on my Blogs. Feel free to reach out via email (chenxinli@link.cuhk.edu.hk) or wechat (jasonchenxinli).
